# Workshop 4: Combat, finalisation and presentations (2 hours)

## Topics
- Attack range and collision checks
- Enemy health/damage
- Integrating and debugging game systems
- Presenting a finished project

## Build in class (first 90 minutes)
1. Add the F-key attack action and cooldown.
2. Detect whether the enemy is within reach and in front of the player.
3. Apply damage and remove a defeated enemy from the game.
4. Integrate and test movement, mining, placing, crafting and combat.

## Final 30 minutes
Students present their games and demonstrate the core mechanics.

## Checkpoint
A playable 2D sandbox with movement, gravity, jumping, mining, building, one crafting recipe, one enemy type and combat.

## Reference files
`SandboxGame.java` (attack and final integration), `Enemy.java`, `Collision.java`.

## Optional extensions (not teaching requirements)
Procedural terrain, extra crafting recipes and items, more complex AI, full inventories, multiplayer and save/load.
