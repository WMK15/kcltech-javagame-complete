# Included game assets

All 12 PNG assets are included under `src/main/resources/assets/`. This workshop's `GameArt` loads the six textures used by its scene without manual setup.

The ten original assets were copied from [WMK15/kcltech-java-game-sandbox](https://github.com/WMK15/kcltech-java-game-sandbox) with its declared Creative Commons CC0 1.0 dedication:
- `blocks/dirt.png`, `blocks/grass.png`, `blocks/stone.png`, `blocks/sand.png`, `blocks/gold.png`, `blocks/wood.png`, `blocks/leaves.png`
- `environment/sky_tile.png`
- `player/idle.png`, `player/step.png`

Two new simple pixel-art sprites are also dedicated CC0 1.0:
- `enemy/slime.png`
- `items/sword.png`

Licence information: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

This workshop renders grass, dirt and stone terrain, sky and two player frames. The other textures remain bundled for later workshops and optional visual customisation.
